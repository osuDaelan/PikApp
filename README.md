# PikApp

A web companion for Pikmin Bloom: track decor Pikmin, explore possible decor locations, compare mushroom rewards, and estimate your level-up progress.

**[Open PikApp](https://osudaelan.github.io/PikApp/)** — works on desktop and mobile, with no login required.

## Decor checklist

- Tap a Pikmin to mark it collected; tap again to uncheck it.
- Rare decor unlocks when its corresponding regular set is complete. Use **Show all** to view locked rare decor too.
- Search by species, decor category, card label or collection status, such as `blue`, `ice cream`, `joker` or `missing`. Decor names work without quotation marks. Open **Search tips** for more options.
- **Shrine & Temple** is optional and off by default. Enable its checkbox in Regular Decor to include the Japan-exclusive set; hiding it preserves your selections.
- When using a missing-items search, newly collected Pikmin remain visible for three seconds so you can undo a misclick.

Regular decor follows the in-game gallery order, with a separate picture and checkbox for each listed design.

Hanafuda, Playing Cards, Mahjong tiles, Rainy Day Leaf Hats, both white/black Chess Piece sets and Theme Park tickets have individual checkboxes. Card tiles show months, suits/ranks or Mahjong tile names. If you tracked them before this update, a review reminder keeps your older grouped selections available while you choose the exact designs you own.

## Decor map

- Choose decor categories, zoom into an area, then select **Search this area**. Results stay visible while you move the map; search again to load another area.
- Turn on **Missing only** to focus on categories you still need in your checklist.
- Use **Add pin**, select a spot on the map, then enter a name, optional notes and categories and choose **Save pin**. Reopen saved places through **My pins** or their map markers.
- Use **Locate me** to move to your location, or explore manually. Location permission is requested only when you choose it.

The map uses OpenStreetMap data to suggest possible locations. Results may differ from what appears in-game.

## Mushroom rewards

- Select the mushrooms available to you in the reference table.
- Set their expected star ratings; new selections default to four stars.
- Adjust quantities to compare combined rewards, such as two small mushrooms against one normal mushroom.
- Choose the nectar colors you want and compare the minimum and maximum rewards to find your best options.

Mushroom comparisons are temporary and reset when you refresh the page.

## Level calculator

Enter your current level, lifetime steps or remaining steps, and remaining flowers. Set your daily step and flower averages—or daily goals—to see estimated level-up dates and coin earnings. The current level data goes through level 120.

## Saving and moving your progress

Calculator settings, decor collection progress and map pins/preferences save automatically in the same browser on the same device. In the calculator stats editor, choose **Save** to apply your edits.

To back up your progress or move it to another browser/device:

1. Open the save-status button in the header and choose **Export progress**.
2. Keep the downloaded file as a backup, or transfer it to the other device.
3. On the destination, choose **Import progress**, review the file, then confirm **Replace browser progress**. Importing replaces the destination save rather than merging it.

Devices do not sync automatically. Clearing browser data can remove your save, so keep an exported backup. Backup files can contain private pin locations and notes; keep them private.

## Feedback

Use **Feedback** in the header to send a private bug report, suggestion or question. Review your message, choose **Continue to send**, then finish any verification in the Formspree tab that opens.
